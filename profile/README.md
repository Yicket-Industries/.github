## About Us

**Yicket Industries™** is an independent technology and engineering project focused on building, experimenting with, and documenting hardware and software.

We work on things like:

- Electronics and embedded systems
- PCB design
- ESP32 / Arduino / Raspberry Pi projects
- Firmware and software
- Operating systems and low-level development
- CAD and 3D modelling
- Prototyping
- Open-source hardware and software

Our goal is simple:

> Build cool things, understand how they work, and share what we learn.

---

## What We Build

### Hardware

PCBs, electronics, prototypes, embedded systems, custom devices and hardware experiments.

### Software & Firmware

Tools, utilities, firmware, system software and software made for our hardware projects.

### CAD & Manufacturing

Enclosures, mechanical parts, printable designs and manufacturing files.

### Research & Experiments

Experimental ideas that may eventually become full Yicket Industries projects.

---

## Open Source

A large part of our work is intended to be publicly documented and open source.

Depending on the project, repositories may include:

- Source code
- Firmware
- Schematics
- PCB files
- CAD files
- BOMs
- Documentation
- Manufacturing files

Each project may use its own license, so check the repository before using or redistributing anything.

## Collaborations

### [Cofeu™](https://github.com/cofeu) × [Yicket Industries™](https://github.com/Yicket-Industries)

Yicket Industries and **Cofeu** collaborate on open-source projects combining hardware and software.

Yicket Industries primarily contributes hardware, electronics, PCB and CAD work, while Cofeu contributes software, infrastructure and development expertise.

Both organizations remain independent.
